Factory baseline

The stock setup from your manual or the manufacturer's instruction sheet. The source is required — future-you trusts numbers with a citation, not from memory.

CV40 reference

Keihin CV40 on 1997–1998 Harley-Davidson Softail (Evolution). Every spec below is tagged: FACTORY means it comes from the factory service manual; COMMUNITY means rider practice, not a factory spec. Nothing here is guessed.

FACTORY Published specs

Source: Harley-Davidson Softail Service Manual #99482-98, Section 4 (Specifications & Fuel System).

COMMUNITY Tuning guide

Rider practice from the Keihin CV community guides — useful, but not factory spec. Verify on your own bike.

FACTORY S&S Super E reference

S&S Super E "Shorty" — the carb on the 1995 Fat Boy. Stock settings and tuning order from S&S's own instruction sheets. Jetting is a starting point only — S&S says so themselves: re-jet for best performance on your bike.

Source: S&S Super E & G Quick Tuning Guide #510-0245, Installation & Jetting Instructions #51-1012, Fuel Line Conversion Kit #510-0279.

FACTORY S&S Super E tuning

S&S's own tuning order and tests (#510-0245), except the mixture-screw diagnostic, which is magazine-sourced and labelled as such.

Not listed here

S&S Super G, Linkert M-series, Mikuni, and every other carb: stock specs vary by application and were not verified for this reference. Record yours from the manufacturer's instruction sheet with the source cited on the baseline — same rule as the CLI.
